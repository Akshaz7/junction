## Recording tips and recovery

- **macOS:** press **Shift–Command–5**, select the app area and record. Hide notifications with Focus, keep document text readable and record narration separately. Check a short playback before the take. See [Apple's Mac recording instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/102618).
- **iOS:** start **Screen Recording** in Control Centre before returning to the native app. Test the AR camera image and voice transcript in playback; use the second phone's audio for the spoken command if microphone capture conflicts with speech input. Transfer the original clip to the Mac. iOS screen recording and screen mirroring cannot run together; choose one capture path. See [Apple's iPhone recording instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/102653).
- **Mirroring/cable capture:** for an operator preview or alternate capture, connect and trust the iPhone, open QuickTime **File → New Movie Recording**, and choose the connected iPhone under Camera. Confirm the preview shows its display before recording. Operate the actual phone for AR. This can replace the native screen recording; keep the second phone filming placement. See [Apple's connected-device capture instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-qa/guide/quicktime-player/qtp356b55534/mac).
- **AR framing:** use a well-lit, textured, non-reflective table. Keep the second phone steady over the operator's shoulder with both the physical table and iPhone display in frame. Avoid glare; move the AR phone slowly and hold after placement.
- **Poor tracking:** try **Reset placement** on a better surface once. Then use a successful physical-iPhone rehearsal clip in the same time slot with **Recorded on iPhone — earlier take** and its document title. If network loading fails, the [offline AR_DEMO AC20 route](DEMO.md#prepare-the-stage) is a separate bundled scene: label it **Offline AC20; voice/sync require connectivity**. Restore the normal build for the main take. If no usable physical clip exists, show the actual laptop 3D view and say **“AR placement was not captured; this is the 3D view.”** Do not composite a fake anchored building. If reveal or voice animation is absent, retain actual placement/highlights and caption the missing effect as pending.
