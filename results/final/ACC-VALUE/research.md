- [scikit-learn: common pitfalls, data leakage](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/common_pitfalls.html#data-leakage): test inputs must remain separate from fitting and preprocessing choices. Our measurement filters the forbidden list before artifact access and exposes only eligible documents to sibling lookup. Fake-provider comparisons cover 200 sample rows and 156 relevant eligible rows, with zero previously correct answers becoming wrong or abstained (`report.json`). These are development measurements, not independent held-out scores.
- [CubiCasa5K official repository](https://github.com/CubiCasa/CubiCasa5k): its README describes separate `train.txt`, `val.txt` and `test.txt` inputs; [upstream training code](https://github.com/CubiCasa/CubiCasa5k/blob/master/train.py) constructs training and validation datasets separately. We preserve the existing splits, train nothing and reserve a random additional eligible file before document inspection. The official split is methodological support; our numeric-field tests do not establish new detector accuracy or a new official split.

No fitted symbol constants were introduced. `constants.json` records inspection of 11 apartment outlines across three eligible CAD files: their outline labels were empty, while 11 room labels and 11 text labels explicitly identified apartments. The implementation uses exact source labels and same-page polygon containment, without a fitted distance or size threshold.
